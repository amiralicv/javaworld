## ✅ Checklist

You wanted data science. Here is the part that is genuinely useful in *every* data job. Do not skip it, do not overdo it.

- [ ] Descriptive stats — mean/median/mode, variance, standard deviation, percentiles (**p50/p95/p99 — you will use these for monitoring, not just analysis**)
- [ ] Distributions — normal, long-tail, and why averages lie
- [ ] Correlation vs causation
- [ ] Sampling and bias
- [ ] Basic probability
- [ ] A/B testing at a concept level — hypothesis, significance, common mistakes

That is enough. If you later go toward ML, you deepen this. If you do not, you have still lost nothing.
