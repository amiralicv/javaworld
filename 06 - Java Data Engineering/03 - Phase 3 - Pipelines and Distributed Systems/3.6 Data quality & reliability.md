## ✅ Checklist

The part that turns "a pipeline exists" into "the business trusts the numbers." **This is the highest-leverage skill in the whole field** and the one most engineers skip.

- [ ] Test categories — freshness, volume, schema, distribution, referential integrity, uniqueness
- [ ] Tools — dbt tests, Great Expectations, Soda
- [ ] **Data contracts** — an agreement with the upstream team about what they will not break
- [ ] Circuit breakers — stop a bad load *before* it reaches the users
- [ ] Reconciliation — proving that what arrived equals what was sent
- [ ] Anomaly detection on metrics
- [ ] Handling the bad row: reject, quarantine, or fail the batch — and how to decide
- [ ] Data lineage — when a number is wrong, how far back does the damage go

> **You are done when…** a bad upstream change causes an alert instead of a business meeting.
