## ✅ Checklist

This is what actually blocks most engineers at the mid-level ceiling. Do not treat it as soft.

- [ ] **Writing** — design docs, RFCs, postmortems, clear README files
- [ ] **Talking to non-technical people** — translating "the pipeline failed" into "yesterday's sales report is 3 hours late, here is the impact"
- [ ] Estimation — and being honest when you do not know
- [ ] Saying no, with an alternative attached
- [ ] Mentoring juniors — teaching is the fastest way to find your own gaps
- [ ] Scoping — cutting a 6-month idea into a 3-week version that delivers value
- [ ] Choosing boring technology on purpose, and defending it

> **The senior test:** people bring you problems, not tasks.
