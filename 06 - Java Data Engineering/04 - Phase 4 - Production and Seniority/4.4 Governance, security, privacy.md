## ✅ Checklist

Boring. Extremely well paid. Especially in banking, which is a large part of the local market.

- [ ] PII classification, masking, tokenization
- [ ] Access control — role-based, row-level, column-level
- [ ] Encryption at rest and in transit
- [ ] Audit logging — who read what, when
- [ ] Retention and deletion policies
- [ ] Data catalog and metadata management (OpenMetadata, DataHub)
- [ ] Regulatory thinking in general — the *habit* of asking "are we allowed to store this?"
